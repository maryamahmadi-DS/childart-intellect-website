How it works

A calm path from a drawing to gentle insights you can revisit later.

1

Upload artwork

Capture a drawing with the camera or choose one from the photo library.

2

Add optional context

Add the child, date, medium, drawing environment, and optional parent notes.

3

Receive observations

Get AI-assisted educational observations grounded in visible features of the drawing.

4

Notice patterns over time

Save artwork and explore recurring themes across multiple analyses.

Main features

Built for parents, legal guardians, and authorized caregivers who want thoughtful reflection—not clinical conclusions.

Artwork analysis

Explore visual elements, composition, possible themes, and gentle reflection prompts.

Child profiles

Keep each child’s artwork organized in a private profile.

Artwork timeline

Browse saved pieces chronologically and revisit past observations.

Visual evidence

Observations point back to what can be seen in the drawing, not unsupported leaps.

Longitudinal insights

After enough successful analyses, notice recurring signals and patterns over time.

Private artwork library

Your family’s drawings stay with your account—not a public social feed.

Responsible interpretation

Drawings can have many meanings. Child Art Intellect provides educational observations and possible interpretations, not diagnoses or clinical conclusions.
