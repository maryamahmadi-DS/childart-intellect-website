Features made for thoughtful parents

Child Art Intellect helps you document artwork, receive gentle insights, and notice patterns over time—without turning creativity into a clinical checklist.

Artwork analysis

Receive AI-assisted educational observations about visual elements, composition, objects, color, possible themes, and contextual notes. Analyses are designed as conversation starters and reflection prompts—not definitive readings of a child.

Visual evidence

Observations should point back to visible elements of the artwork. Child Art Intellect emphasizes what can be seen on the page, helping keep interpretations grounded and avoiding unsupported conclusions.

Child profiles

Organize artwork separately for each child. Profiles make it easier to keep memories, timelines, and observations clearly associated with the right young artist.

Timeline

Browse saved artwork chronologically. Revisit earlier observations, compare pieces side by side in your mind, and notice how expression evolves across time.

Longitudinal insights

After enough successful analyses, Child Art Intellect can summarize recurring signals and patterns over time—useful for curiosity and gentle noticing, not for labeling or scoring a child.

Privacy

Artwork and child profiles remain associated with your private account. Child Art Intellect is not presented as a public social network for sharing children’s drawings.

ChildArt Plus

Free includes 3 lifetime analyses and 1 child profile. ChildArt Plus expands to 30 analyses per month, up to 5 child profiles, and longitudinal insights after sufficient analyses. Monthly and yearly plans are available through Apple In-App Purchase.
